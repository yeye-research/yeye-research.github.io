Research interests

I study how automated systems decide whether a sentence is supported by a source, and what those decisions silently commit their operators to.

  • Citation verification and attribution evaluation
  • Threshold audits and cost-sensitive decision making
  • Non-identifiability of lexical-overlap metrics
  • Reliability of human annotation in evaluation
  • Reproducibility of empirical claims

Current work

A full audit of one citation-checking pipeline: 308 sentences under review, 1,803 constructed negative pairs, 46 source documents, and 100 blind human labels. The central result is negative — substituting a set-overlap index for a threshold sweep does not work — and the paper reports the two consequences that result forces.
